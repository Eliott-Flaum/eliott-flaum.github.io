About

I'm a postdoctoral fellow working at the intersection of [your field / research area]. My work looks at [one or two sentences on what you study now and why it matters].

Before this, I completed my PhD in the Prakash Lab at Stanford, where I spent several years studying Lacrymaria olor — a single-celled organism that can shoot a "neck" more than thirty times its own body length in under a second. Working with Manu Prakash, we found the geometric mechanism behind that motion: a helical array of microtubule ribbons wrapped around the cell, acting like a collapsible, self-unfurling scaffold. The work appeared in Science, with cover art my friend [friend's name] helped bring to life.
